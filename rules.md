Welcome to the **MTL City Rp** official laws and regulations handbook. To maintain an immersive, respectful, and high-performance roleplay environment across Los Santos and our Discord hub, every citizen is strictly expected to know and obey these regulations.

---

## ⬇️ Server Rules & Regulations (Discord & Community)

These rules apply to all community communication channels, Discord servers, voice rooms, and in-game OOC discussions:

1. **Be Respectful:** Treat everyone with kindness and dignity. Absolutely no harassment, targeted bullying, racism, bigotry, or hate speech.
2. **No Spam:** Do not spam text messages, emojis, mention tags, or soundboards in voice channels.
3. **No NSFW:** This community strictly operates as a Safe-For-Work (SFW) server. Absolutely no pornographic, sexually explicit, or gruesome content.
4. **No Advertising:** Do not self-promote, share unauthorized invite links, or advertise other Discord servers, game servers, or websites without formal executive permission.
5. **Follow Discord TOS:** All citizens must strictly abide by Discord’s Official Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.
6. **Listen to Staff:** Respectfully follow instructions from our moderators, administrators, and developer team at all times.

## 3. Detailed Roleplay Explanations & Protocols

### 3.1 Realistic Citizen Values (Value of Life / NVL)
- **Value Your Life (NVL):** Your character must realistically fear injury and death. If a firearm is pointed directly at you or you are cornered by multiple armed attackers, you must surrender and cooperate with reasonable IC demands.
- **Hero Roleplay:** Unrealistic bravery in lethal situations is strictly prohibited. Never attempt to draw a weapon or initiate combat while held at gunpoint.

### 3.2 Combat & Vehicle Ethics
- **RDM (Random Deathmatch):** Engaging in lethal force or attacking another citizen without prior verbal, In-Character interaction and authentic motivation is illegal.
- **VDM (Vehicle Deathmatch):** Intentionally ramming or running over citizens using a motorized vehicle as a primary weapon without unavoidable RP context is strictly banned.
- **New Life Rule (NLR):** Upon full respawn at a medical facility after emergency services fail to revive you, you lose all memory of your fatal encounter. You cannot return to the death zone or rejoin that scenario for at least **30 minutes**, nor seek immediate revenge.

### 3.3 Crime & Emergency Immunity
- **Cop Baiting:** Doing intentional illegal maneuvers or revving engines in front of police officers purely to initiate prolonged high-speed chases without real IC motivation is forbidden.
- **EMS & Government Protection:** Paramedics actively treating wounded citizens on hospital grounds or active medical triage sites are strictly protected from kidnapping, robbery, or assault.

> **Contesting a Ruling or Reporting a Violation?** Connect to our live community moderation team immediately via the **Discord Hub** on our homepage!
