Welcome to the **MTL City Rp** official laws and community code of conduct. To maintain an authentic, immersive, and enjoyable roleplay environment for every citizen in Los Santos, all players are expected to understand and abide by these rules.

---

## 1. Core Roleplay Standards

### 1.1 In-Character (IC) vs. Out-Of-Character (OOC)
- **Always Stay In-Character:** Once you spawn into MTL City, you must remain in-character at all times during active roleplay situations. 
- **OOC Chat usage:** Local or global OOC chats should be strictly reserved for technical support or reporting game-breaking issues. Never argue or disrupt IC scenarios using OOC communication.
- **Microphone Requirement:** A working, clearly audible headset or microphone is strictly required to participate in MTL City Rp.

### 1.2 Realistic Citizen Values (Value of Life / NVL)
- **Value Your Life (NVL):** Characters must reasonably fear death and injury. If a weapon is pointed directly at you or you are heavily outnumbered by assailants, you must surrender and follow reasonable RP instructions.
- **Super-Cop or Hero RP:** Unrealistic bravery in lethal situations is prohibited. Do not draw a weapon while held at gunpoint.

---

## 2. Prohibited Behavior & Violations

### 2.1 Random Deathmatch & Vehicle Deathmatch
- **RDM (Random Deathmatch):** Killing, harming, or attempting to assault another player without prior valid in-character verbal interaction and realistic motivation is strictly prohibited.
- **VDM (Vehicle Deathmatch):** Using a motorized vehicle intentionally as a primary weapon against pedestrians or other vehicles without valid, unavoidable RP context is strictly banned.

### 2.2 Metagaming & Powergaming
- **Metagaming:** Utilizing outside real-world information (such as Twitch streams, Discord direct messages, or interactive web rosters) during active in-character situations. Your character only knows what they directly observe or learn inside the game world.
- **Powergaming:** Forcing roleplay outcomes onto another player without granting them a realistic chance to react or counter (e.g. `/me strips all weapons and binds arms silently`). Also includes using physical impossible exploits or shortcuts.

### 2.3 New Life Rule (NLR)
- Upon total incapacitation and respawning at a hospital facility (meaning emergency medical services could not revive your character at the scene):
  1. You lose all memory of the direct altercation that led to your death.
  2. You cannot return to the area of your death or rejoin that ongoing scenario for at least **30 minutes**.
  3. You cannot initiate revenge actions against the parties involved in your fatal accident.

---

## 3. Crime & Law Enforcement Interactions

- **Hostage Protocols:** Taking fake hostages (friends pretending to be hostages) to generate easy robberies is strictly unlawful. Real hostages must receive engaging, dynamic verbal RP.
- **Cop Baiting:** Intentionally performing deliberate illegal activities in front of law enforcement solely to initiate high-speed vehicle chases without authentic motivation is forbidden.
- **Government & Medical Immunity:** Paramedics and medical personnel treating wounded citizens at official hospital grounds or neutral emergency zones are strictly protected from kidnapping or assault during active rescue duties.

---

## 4. Community & Discord Etiquette

- **Respect All Citizens:** Zero tolerance exists for racism, bigotry, sexual harassment, targeted out-of-character bullying, or hate speech across both our FiveM server and Discord community.
- **Staff Instructions:** Decisions made by Senior Leads, Admins, and Server Owners during moderation tickets or support inquiries must be adhered to respectfully. If you wish to contest a ruling, open an formal inquiry ticket in our Discord Hub.

> **Need Support?** If you witness rule violations or need developer assistance, connect to our live staff team immediately via the **Discord Hub** on our homepage!
